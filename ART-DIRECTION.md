# Current scroll prototype

The current scene uses authored real-time geometry, procedural material textures, 3D foliage, procedural cloud sprites, and locally bundled team character textures. It does not use fullscreen background paintings or image crossfades.

Morning palette: muted teal train and roof metal, warm cream bodywork, weathered wood, cool green shadows, hydrangeas, and distant mountain silhouettes.

The older generated paintings under `dist/assets/` are unused remnants of the previous direction. The exact prompts and earlier implementation were part of the superseded eight-station version; they are not the source of the current world.
